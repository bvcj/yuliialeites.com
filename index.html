ABOUT

Yuliia Leites is a psychoanalytic psychotherapist from Kyiv, Ukraine, and a candidate of the International Psychoanalytical Association (IPA).

Her clinical work moves between the intimate and the historical: anxiety, depression, trauma, relationship difficulties, questions of desire, identity, loss, exile, and the strange pressure of staying alive inside unstable times.

She works with civilians, soldiers, queer people, and those who do not arrive with a clear category — only with symptoms, stories, repetitions, dreams, and the difficult fact of having an inner life.

As a member of the Ukrainian Psychedelic Research Association (UPRA), she is interested in how psychoanalytic thinking can meet altered states, trauma, and new forms of care without becoming shallow, hygienic, or too sure of itself.
