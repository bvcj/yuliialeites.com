ABOUT

Yuliia Leites is a psychoanalytic psychotherapist from Kyiv, Ukraine,
and a candidate of the International Psychoanalytical Association
(IPA).

Her practice sits at the fracture points of war and social upheaval —
with civilians, soldiers
and queer people.

As a member of the Ukrainian Psychedelic Research Association (UPRA),
she brings psychoanalytic thinking into psychedelic-assisted therapy —
working at the intersection of trauma, language,
body, and collective experience.
A radical method for a radical age.
